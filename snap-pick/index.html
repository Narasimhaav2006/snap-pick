The process

From a quiet request to a clean doorstep

Collecting waste fairly is mostly a problem of timing and weight. Snap & Pick takes care of both, so that every vehicle leaves full but never overloaded, and every household knows exactly when to expect us.

Request a pickup

  1. 01

    Tell us what you're letting go

    Fill in a short form with the kind of waste you have (wet, dry, hazardous, electronic or bulky), roughly how many kilograms, your address and the day that suits you. A quick photo helps the collector know what to expect. If you would like, we will also send WhatsApp updates as your pickup progresses.

    Takes under a minute

  2. 02

    We gather your neighbours' requests

    Requests from the same area and the same date are brought together, so one vehicle can serve a whole street in a single trip rather than many scattered ones. Fewer journeys mean quieter roads and a lighter footprint for everyone.

    Grouped by area and date

  3. 03

    A slot that always fits

    Every vehicle carries a fixed load, and the scheduler never goes past it. When a day is full, your request quietly moves to the next available slot. If nothing is free, you are placed on a waitlist and promoted automatically the moment space opens up, with no need to ask again.

    Capacity-aware, never overloaded

  4. 04

    Your driver is on the way

    The collector receives an ordered route with addresses, photos and phone numbers, laid out to keep the distance short. You are told who is coming and in which truck, and if you have opted in, we send a message once your driver is close, so you can bring your waste out in time.

    Route-optimised, with live alerts

  5. 05

    Collected, or gently rescheduled

    Once the pickup is done, it is marked as collected and you can see it on your tracker. If something goes wrong and a pickup is missed, the dispatcher puts it back on the schedule for a new date. You can also cancel at any time before the truck arrives.

    Every status, tracked end to end
